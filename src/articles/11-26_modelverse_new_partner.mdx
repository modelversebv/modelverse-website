## What to expect from David’s role at Modelverse

David's addition is our first step in a new phase of expansion. David will work with the team on strategy, proposition development, partnerships and market growth. A central part of his role is helping organizations bridge and solidify the connection between board responsibility on cybersecurity and governance and on the other hand the operational responsible teams that take care of the controls, evidence and daily work.

## First a client, now a Partner

David knows Modelverse from the customer's side. As a former client, he used Modelverse as a security management platform and spoke publicly about the value it added. He therefore joins with direct experience of what it takes to manage security and compliance across an international, multi-site organisation.

## His background

His career began in logistics and information management. It went on to include enterprise software companies such as Unit4, Enablon, as well as interim management assignments at international software companies. That range gives him a view of cybersecurity from several positions: the executive who answers for the risk, the technology function that manages it, and the business that has to keep running while it does.

## David's motto

Organisations are facing increasing demands around cyber resilience, digital sovereignty, regulatory compliance and third-party risk. Yet responsibility for these subjects is often divided across leadership, technology, risk, compliance and assurance teams. Modelverse believes the answer is not another isolated framework or tool, but an operating model that connects decisions, controls, evidence and execution.

**“What attracted me to Modelverse is the ambition to go beyond traditional compliance and GRC. Cybersecurity and governance are ultimately management disciplines. They require an operating model that connects boardroom priorities with what actually happens across technology, operations and assurance”** says David Warnink.

In the coming period, David will concentrate on three areas. He will further sharpen the Modelverse proposition, so that it is clear which problems the platform solves and for whom. He will develop the partner ecosystem, working with the advisory and assurance firms that support their own clients through Modelverse. And he will also support customers who want to turn complex cyber and governance requirements into one coherent way of working.
